## S03 feature-callout

MEET THE MODELS
### Get familiar with Nano Banana 2 and GPT Image 2.0
Both Nano Banana 2 and GPT Image 2.0 turn a prompt into a finished image, but with different strengths. Nano Banana 2 is Google's Flash-speed model built on Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, delivering near-Pro quality up to 4K fast. GPT Image 2.0 is OpenAI's autoregressive model built for ~99% text accuracy and studio-grade photorealism. Generate with both in our AI Playground.

## S04 feature-callout

SPEED & RESOLUTION
### Compare speed, resolution, and cost
Both models are fast: Nano Banana 2 generates at Flash speed with up to 40% lower cost, while GPT Image 2.0 returns an image in about three seconds - 3 to 5x faster than GPT Image 1.5. On resolution, Nano Banana 2 reaches full 4K from 512px, and GPT Image 2.0 generates up to 2048x2048 natively with 4K available in beta. For high-volume speed, Nano Banana 2 leads; for native text-perfect output, GPT Image 2.0 leads.
![image creative 480x480: Nano Banana 2 vs GPT Image 2.0 speed and resolution](media/9e0a38de-ab11-4fdb-8871-1b223d100ab4-873f1e42.webp "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/9e0a38de-ab11-4fdb-8871-1b223d100ab4.webp?type=webp&to=min&r=1200&q=90")

## S05 feature-callout

TEXT & TYPOGRAPHY
### Text accuracy and multilingual rendering
This is where GPT Image 2.0 stands out: ~99% character-level text accuracy across headlines, fine print, and multilingual labels - Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Hebrew - making it ideal for posters, packaging, and infographics. Nano Banana 2 also renders text well and pairs it with Gemini's search-grounded accuracy. If typography is the job, GPT Image 2.0 is the pick.
![image creative 480x480: Nano Banana 2 vs GPT Image 2.0 text rendering](media/a6439ac4-c803-4339-be4a-f96469e3c736-fe42c0ef.avif "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/a6439ac4-c803-4339-be4a-f96469e3c736.avif")

## S06 feature-callout

EDITING & CONSISTENCY
### Editing, consistency, and provenance
GPT Image 2.0 edits through plain language - inpainting, outpainting, object removal, background replacement, and style transfer - keeps up to 8 consistent images per prompt, and embeds C2PA content credentials for provenance. Nano Banana 2 maintains up to five characters and fourteen objects per scene with strong consistency, editable inside Picsart's tools. Both scale well; GPT Image 2.0 adds deeper editing and provenance.
