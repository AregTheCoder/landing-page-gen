## S04 feature-callout

REUSABLE STYLES
### Build a reusable style from your references
Recraft V4 Styles turns 1 to 10 reference images into a reusable style you can apply to any prompt. Instead of copying a surface look, it captures texture, composition, and rendering and holds them steady across every generation. References can come from anywhere - other generators, branding materials, or existing assets - with no training or fine-tuning required. Similar references sharpen the match; diverse ones widen the range.
![image creative 480x480: Build a reusable style with Recraft V4 Styles](https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/52bc1e66-3fbc-48d5-a098-906463e7fb9d.avif)

## S05 feature-callout

PRECISE OR FLEXIBLE
### Set how close the match is - Precise or Flexible
Two style-match modes put you in control. Precise, the default, follows your reference meticulously - down to texture and rendering - and it's the mode that was preferred in 91.6% of blind, third-party-judged matchups across 159 styles. Flexible matches the general vibe instead - closer to the styles experience from V3, refined for V4 - when you want room to interpret. Attach your style, write a prompt, and generate in the aspect ratio you need.
![image creative 480x480: Recraft V4 Styles Precise and Flexible modes](https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/588c0260-e801-40a9-a7b1-468648f7c859.avif)
