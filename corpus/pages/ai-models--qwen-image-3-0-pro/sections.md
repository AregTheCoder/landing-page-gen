## S04 feature-callout

MEET QWEN IMAGE 3.0 PRO
### What makes Qwen Image 3.0 Pro flagship
Qwen Image 3.0 Pro (also written Qwen Image 3 Pro) is Alibaba's flagship image model - a generation beyond Qwen 2. It combines high-fidelity text-to-image with prompt-based editing, and adds two intelligence layers: prompt-rewrite (direct or agent-based) to expand a short brief, and a thinking mode that reasons through complex prompts before generating. Output runs up to 2048px with strong typography and photoreal detail for editorial and brand work.

### Legible text, rendered right
Where many image models garble small type, Qwen Image 3.0 Pro renders text you can actually read - clean lettering and accurate spelling for posters, packaging, ads, social graphics, and UI mockups. Its prompt-rewrite and thinking modes help it hold long, structured briefs together, so headlines, labels, and captions land where you place them instead of turning to mush.
