## S03 feature-callout

TURBO-SPEED TIER
### MiniMax H3 Max Turbo at a glance
MiniMax H3 Max Turbo is the turbo-speed tier of MiniMax H3, built for faster-than-real-time generation. It creates video from text or an image, supports start and end frames, and lets you set prompt expansion to balanced or quality, add a seed for reproducible runs, and pick from 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, or 9:16. Output runs at 480p or 768p, from 5 to 15 seconds.

## S06 feature-callout

PRECISION CONTROLS
### Frames, prompt expansion, and a seed
Turbo gives you the same hands-on controls as the rest of the family: set a start and end frame to lock how a shot opens and closes, then choose a prompt-expansion mode - balanced or quality - to decide how much the model elaborates on your prompt. Add a seed for reproducible results, pick any aspect ratio from 21:9 to 9:16, and set any length from 5 to 15 seconds at 480p or 768p.
