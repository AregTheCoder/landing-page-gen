Picsart Flow FAQ
[Try for free](https://picsart.com/create/workflows)
01
What is Picsart Flow?
Picsart Flow is an AI workflow tool that lets you ideate, iterate, and automate designs on a single visual canvas. It’s a no-code workflow builder for solo creators or teams, making creative workflow automation simple and powerful.
02
How does Picsart Flow work?
Start with your own content, a quick prompt, or one of Picsart’s workflow templates. Connect images, videos, text, and sound to create stunning designs, then package it all as an AI workflow generator that you can share or reuse anytime.
03
Can I collaborate with my team in Picsart Flow?
Yes! Picsart Flow offers real-time collaboration so you and your team can co-create on the infinite canvas. Share your workflows, chat directly in the tool, and build collaborative design workflows anywhere, with anyone.
04
Is Picsart Flow free to use?
Yes - you can start creating with AI design workflows using AI Credits. AI Credits come included with all Picsart subscriptions, and you can also purchase them as one-off packs.
05
What kind of workflows can I create?
With creative workflow automation, the possibilities are endless. Design ads, create branded assets, localize campaigns, remix visuals into videos, or build your brand identity from scratch.
