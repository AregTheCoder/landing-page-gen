## S12 faq

### CV maker FAQ
#### What is a CV?
A curriculum vitae (CV) is a comprehensive document describing an individual’s professional experience, skills, academic background, and achievements. A CV is typically more thorough than a traditional resume because its purpose is to demonstrate extensive qualifications. While CVs are most common in academic roles, research careers, and international jobs, they are often requested for any role requiring a lengthy and/or highly specialized work history.
