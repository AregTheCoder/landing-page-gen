## AI audio models used by Picsart
Picsart brings together the world's most powerful AI audio models — including ElevenLabs, Google, Lyria 3, Gemini TTS, MiniMax Music, and more — in one creative platform. Whether you're generating natural text-to-speech voiceovers, composing original music from a prompt, creating custom sound effects, or dubbing and reshaping voices, each model is purpose-built to push the boundaries of what AI audio can do. Explore, experiment, and create — all without switching tools.
