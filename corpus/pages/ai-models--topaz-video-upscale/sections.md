## S03 feature-callout

### Bring any video up to crisp 4K
Topaz Video Upscale, from Topaz Labs, rebuilds resolution and detail in footage you already have. Working frame by frame, it reconstructs texture and edges — turning soft, low-res, or dated clips into sharp HD and 4K that look like they were captured that way. No reshoot and no source files needed: just a cleaner, higher-resolution version of the video you started with.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/b950e4d6-0983-4286-b560-8ad332c203f6-4239d80e.webm "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/b950e4d6-0983-4286-b560-8ad332c203f6.webm")

## S04 feature-callout

### Erase noise, grain, and compression artifacts
Beyond raw resolution, Topaz Video Upscale removes the flaws that give footage away — sensor noise, grain, blocky compression, and motion blur. It deblurs soft frames and rebuilds fine edges without introducing shimmer or flicker between frames, so the entire clip stays stable. The output reads as genuinely higher quality, not just a larger, softer copy of the original.

### Enhancement that respects motion and color
What separates Topaz Video Upscale from a simple resize is fidelity: it holds the original timing, camera motion, and color intact while adding detail, so nothing warps or drifts. It handles everything from old home videos and archival reels to screen recordings and social clips — which is why creators reach for it to rescue and future-proof footage instead of shooting it again.
