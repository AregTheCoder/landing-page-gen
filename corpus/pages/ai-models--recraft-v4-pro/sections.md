## S07 feature-callout

### Why creators choose Recraft V4 Pro
Recraft V4 Pro is the upgrade path within the V4 family. Use Recraft V4 for everyday design work; switch to V4 Pro when the output has to be brand-campaign ready. Sharper detail (logo crispness, typography accuracy, edge fidelity), better composition on multi-element briefs, and stronger prompt fidelity on technical specs (color systems, layout grids) make it the right pick for designers shipping client work.
