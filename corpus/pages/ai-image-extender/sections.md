### Inpaint and outpaint images with AI
AI inpainting is the process of filling in missing pixels in an image, while AI outpainting is the process of extending the image beyond its original boundaries. This means that you can easily adjust the aspect ratio of your images without having to crop or stretch them. Works perfectly for turning portraits into YouTube thumbnails, Etsy shop covers, and more!
