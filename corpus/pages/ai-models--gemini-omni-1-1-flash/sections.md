## S03 feature-callout

EVERY INPUT, ONE MODEL
### Turn text, images, and video into one Gemini AI video
Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash is genuinely multimodal: start from a text prompt, a single image, or existing footage, and combine them in one generation. Feed up to 5 reference images to lock characters, products, or style, and up to 3 reference videos to carry motion and look across shots - so every clip stays on-brand from the first frame to the last.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/fc9d3c9a-992e-4777-875f-55b696a088e2-bf2a45cf.webm "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/fc9d3c9a-992e-4777-875f-55b696a088e2.webm")

## S04 feature-callout

START & END FRAME CONTROL
### Set the first and last frame, interpolate everything between
Pin a start frame and an end frame and Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash fills in the motion between them, so a transition lands exactly where you want it. It's frame-level control most text-to-video models don't give you - ideal for logo reveals, product turns, and seamless loops - with output from 360p all the way up to 4K, in 16:9 or 9:16.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/738cfdc7-2fbe-425c-858f-aecbf34677ff-418d4fce.webm "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/738cfdc7-2fbe-425c-858f-aecbf34677ff.webm")

## S05 feature-callout

UP TO 4K OUTPUT
### Google video generation, sharp enough for up to 4K
Every Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash clip renders up to native 4K, in 16:9 for landscape or 9:16 for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok - no upscaling or reformatting. Generate up to 10 seconds per clip, then extend the result when a scene needs more room to breathe.
