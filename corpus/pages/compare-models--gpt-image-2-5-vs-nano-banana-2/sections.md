## S03 feature-callout

MEET THE MODELS
### Get familiar with GPT Image 2.5 and Nano Banana 2
At a glance, both GPT Image 2.5 (GPT 2.5) and Nano Banana 2 turn a prompt into a high-quality image, but with different priorities. GPT 2.5 is OpenAI's precision model - sharp detail, ~99% text accuracy, and exact multi-turn editing. Nano Banana 2 is Google's speed model built on Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, delivering near-Pro quality at Flash speed. Try them both in our AI playground and AI Image Generator .

## S04 feature-callout

QUALITY & TEXT
### Compare detail, text accuracy, and resolution
GPT Image 2.5 leads on precision: the sharpest detail, ~99% text accuracy for legible headlines and small type, and up to 4K output. Nano Banana 2 renders advanced text with in-image localization for multilingual visuals and reaches 4K too, prioritizing dynamic lighting and speed. If exact text and maximum fidelity matter most, GPT Image 2.5 leads; for fast multilingual visuals, Nano Banana 2 shines.
![image creative 480x480: GPT Image 2.5 vs Nano Banana 2 detail and text](media/2a8d68c7-5656-490b-ac11-dd1d07d52b52-97eee867.avif "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/2a8d68c7-5656-490b-ac11-dd1d07d52b52.avif")

## S05 feature-callout

SPEED & COST
### Flash speed vs precision passes
Nano Banana 2 is built for throughput - Flash-speed generation at up to 40% lower API cost, ideal for high-volume prototyping and social content. GPT Image 2.5 offers a fast Flare variant (2-4x quicker than GPT Image 2) plus a precision Sunburst variant when quality outweighs turnaround. Choose Nano Banana 2 for volume and speed; choose GPT Image 2.5 when you want a speed-or-precision dial.
![image creative 480x480: GPT Image 2.5 vs Nano Banana 2 speed and cost](media/a90b63ab-64e4-4b4f-a541-cdec37abc167-0da70bbd.webp "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/a90b63ab-64e4-4b4f-a541-cdec37abc167.webp")

## S06 feature-callout

EDITING & REFERENCES
### Editing precision and reference control
GPT Image 2.5 accepts up to 16 reference images and holds the rest of the frame exactly during single-element edits, with multi-turn stability so long sessions don't degrade. Nano Banana 2 keeps up to 5 characters and 14 objects consistent across a scene and can pull real web images as context via Grounding with Google Image Search. GPT Image 2.5 favors surgical edits; Nano Banana 2 favors consistent multi-subject scenes.
