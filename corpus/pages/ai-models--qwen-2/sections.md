## S04 feature-callout

### What is Qwen 2?
Qwen 2 is Alibaba's text-to-image model in the Qwen family. It reads natural-language prompts and produces photoreal images with reliable composition, accurate lighting, and clean detail. Qwen 2 sits at the entry tier of the Qwen image family — lower credit cost than Qwen 2 Pro, and distinct from the Qwen Image multimodal flagship. It is built for creators who need solid everyday output without the premium price point.

## S07 feature-callout

### Why creators choose Qwen 2
Qwen 2 is the entry tier of the Qwen image family — and that's its strength. Low credit cost makes it the right pick for exploratory drafts, social content, and high-volume creator workflows where Pro-tier polish isn't required. Strong prompt fidelity keeps output close to what you asked for, and clean composition means you spend less time re-prompting. When the project graduates to editorial or print, upgrade to Qwen 2 Pro from the same model selector.
