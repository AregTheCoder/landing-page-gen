## S03 feature-callout

### Meet Lyria 3.5: where ideas become complete tracks
Lyria 3.5 is Google DeepMind’s latest AI music generation model, built to create richer and more expressive tracks from simple creative direction. Describe the genre, mood, instruments, vocal style, tempo, or duration you want, and the model turns those details into a cohesive piece of music. Compared with earlier versions, Lyria 3.5 offers stronger musicality, more natural vocals, better lyric generation, and greater control over how a track develops. It can create everything from quick musical clips to full three-minute songs, helping creators produce audio that better fits the length and mood of their projects.
