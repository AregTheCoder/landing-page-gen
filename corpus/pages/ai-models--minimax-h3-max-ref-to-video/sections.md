## S03 feature-callout

REFERENCE-DRIVEN VARIANT
### MiniMax H3 Max Ref-to-Video at a glance
MiniMax H3 Max Ref-to-Video is the reference-driven member of the MiniMax H3 Max family. Instead of starting from a blank prompt, you feed it multiple reference images, video clips, and audio files, then describe how to use them. It generates a new clip of 5 to 15 seconds at 480p or 768p, in adaptive or fixed aspect ratios from 21:9 to 9:16 - grounding the result in the material you provide.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/0b46c11f-8a3a-458e-b012-97fc34d2f829-52de21d3.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/0b46c11f-8a3a-458e-b012-97fc34d2f829.webm")

## S04 feature-callout

CITE BY INDEX
### Reference images, clips, and audio right in your prompt
Ref-to-Video reads the references you attach in order and lets you point to them directly in the prompt - Image 1, Video 1, Audio 1, and so on. Pull a character from one image, a motion or setting from a video clip, and a voice or soundtrack from an audio file, then describe how they should come together. The model composes a single coherent video that honors each reference, so your source material shapes the shot instead of a text description alone.

## S06 feature-callout

ONE COHERENT CLIP
### Many references in, one on-brief clip out
Ref-to-Video is built for control: attach as many images, clips, and audio files as your shot needs, then use the prompt to direct exactly how each is used. Choose 480p or 768p, set a length from 5 to 15 seconds, and pick an adaptive ratio or a fixed one from 21:9 to 9:16. The result stays grounded in your material while the model handles motion and continuity - so your references become a finished video, not a mood board.
