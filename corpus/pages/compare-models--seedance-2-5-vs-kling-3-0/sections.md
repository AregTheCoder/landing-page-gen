## S04 feature-callout

MEET THE MODELS
### Get familiar with Seedance 2.5 and Kling 3.0
At a glance, both Seedance 2.5 and Kling 3.0 turn a prompt into high-quality video, but with different creative priorities. Seedance 2.5 is built for continuous, cinematic single-shot storytelling - up to 30 seconds in one take with physics-accurate light and synced audio. Kling 3.0 leans into structured, multi-shot control with longer sequences and hands-on motion tools. Try them both in our AI Video Generator .

## S05 feature-callout

LENGTH, RESOLUTION, AUDIO
### Compare clip length, resolution, and audio
Seedance 2.5 holds a full 30-second continuous shot with matched audio and 20-language lip-sync - ideal when you want one unbroken scene. Kling 3.0 caps single clips near 15 seconds but extends across scenes to minutes, outputs native 4K at up to 60fps, and adds 16-bit HDR/EXR for pro finishing. If unbroken length and synced dialogue matter most, Seedance leads; for maximum resolution and HDR, Kling leads.
![video creative 480x480: ](https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/9ab0b781-8748-4cfd-91d7-6ad502d5e502.webm)

## S06 feature-callout

STORY VS CONTROL
### One continuous take vs multi-shot control
Kling 3.0 gives you hands-on direction - multi-shot storyboarding across up to six connected scenes, Motion Brush, and camera-path control. Seedance 2.5 takes the opposite approach: one continuous 30-second take that needs no stitching, with physics-accurate lighting and impact physics keeping the action believable. Kling is manual control; Seedance is effortless continuity.
![video creative 480x480: ](https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/8671b298-7ee3-44a8-87ed-c4eebfd417a6.webm)

## S07 feature-callout

CONSISTENCY & REFERENCES
### References, character consistency, and control
Seedance 2.5 accepts up to 50 references - images, video, and audio - to lock a character's face, wardrobe, and voice across an entire sequence, plus 3D-reference VFX and texturing. Kling 3.0 uses reference extraction for visual and voice traits. Both keep characters consistent; Seedance offers more reference headroom, while Kling offers more shot-level control.
![video creative 480x480: ](https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/ac5aa2a5-f623-44a5-b4f4-32471a194c41.webm)
