## S03 feature-callout

### What is Kling Video O1 AI?
Kling Video O1 is a unified multimodal video model that brings different video tasks into one place. It can generate, transform, reference, and continue video using images, existing clips, saved subjects, and text as inputs. Instead of switching between separate modes for each task, you can combine different instructions in the same prompt and shape the video you want from there.

## S05 feature-callout

### Keep the same characters across scenes
Kling Video O1 can use reference images to keep a character, product, or object consistent as you create new shots. Change the camera angle, movement, or scene, and the model works to preserve how your subject looks. You can also reference multiple characters or objects at once, making it easier to create a sequence without their appearance changing from shot to shot.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/02ca2771-a079-4c3c-80d3-e7e25329ab32-270072f1.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/02ca2771-a079-4c3c-80d3-e7e25329ab32.webm")

## S06 feature-callout

### Generate, edit, and continue your video
Video O1 goes beyond generating a new clip. Describe what you want to change - remove something from the shot, switch the background, restyle the scene, change the weather, or adjust an object - without manually masking or setting keyframes. You can also give it an existing video and generate the shot before or after it, or use the clip’s action or camera movement as a reference for something new.
