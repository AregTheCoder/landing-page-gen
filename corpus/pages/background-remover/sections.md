#### What does the Background Remover do?
A Background Remover tool uses artificial intelligence (AI) algorithms to analyze the pixels in an image. It differentiates the foreground (main subject) and the background. The tool then removes the background pixels while preserving the edges and details of the foreground. You can download the image with a transparent background or replace the background with something new.
