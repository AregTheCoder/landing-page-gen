### What is Seedream 4.5?
Seedream 4.5 is ByteDance Seed’s newest AI image generation model. It is a generative image system that unifies image generation and image editing into a single workflow, built for fast iteration and consistency across creative variations. It excels at dense text rendering, character consistency across multiple images, and natural-language image editing with up to 14 reference inputs — making it ideal for professional creators who need control and consistency. ## S07 feature-callout

### Why creators choose Seedream 4.5
Seedream 4.5 combines generation and editing in a single model, eliminating the need to switch between separate tools. Its dense text rendering is among the best available — handling headlines, small fonts, and multilingual labels with precision that most competitors cannot match. Character consistency across multiple outputs means campaigns stay visually cohesive. And at approximately $0.04 per image, it delivers roughly 4x better cost efficiency than competing models, making it practical for high-volume professional workflows.
