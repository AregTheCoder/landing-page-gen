When it comes to photography, lighting is crucial. It’s what separates the great shots from the not-so-great shots. Experimenting with light can completely transform a simple photo into something extraordinary. A fluorescent light can appear harsh and lifeless, while a golden light can imbue warmth and beauty on an image. Photographs with low light levels can appear moody or injoke an air of mystery, while those with brighter light can elevate happy, summery, hidden details.
