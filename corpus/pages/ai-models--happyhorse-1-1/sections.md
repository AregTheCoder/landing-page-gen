## S04 feature-callout

### What is the Happy Horse 1.1 AI model?
Happy Horse 1.1 is a unified text-to-video AI model that turns a written prompt into a finished clip. Built on a single transformer that processes text, image, and audio together, it generates video up to 15 seconds at 1080p with lifelike motion and natively synchronized audio — no separate dubbing step. It supports multilingual lip-sync, so spoken scenes stay accurate across languages.
