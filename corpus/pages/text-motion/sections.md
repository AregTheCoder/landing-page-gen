## S06 feature-callout

### What is Text Motion and how does it work?
Text Motion is an AI-powered text animation tool that turns written headlines into animated motion video clips. It focuses on intelligent typography motion, not cinematic scene generation or script writing. You simply animate text online to amplify existing messaging, without using a full video production suite.
![video creative 569x505: ](https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/9e4495cf-85ec-4b1f-b938-4425b7f30aec.webm)

## S08 feature-callout

### Create text animation without After Effects
Traditional animation tools demand keyframes, timeline scrubbing, motion curves, and constant manual adjustments. That process takes time and technical skill. Text Motion replaces that complexity with automated motion logic and optimized movement patterns. You create text animation without After Effects, timelines, or motion design experience. Just enter text and generate polished results instantly.
