## S03 feature-callout

SHARPER OUTPUT
### Sharper detail, more natural light
GPT Image 2.5 Flare renders sharper details, more natural lighting, and richer textures than Images 2.0. Subjects from reference photos are preserved more faithfully, and images stay clear and realistic even after multiple rounds of retouching. Infographic accuracy and layout improved measurably - though very dense small text can still take more than one attempt.
![image creative 480x480: GPT Image 2.5 sharper detail and texture](media/2a8d68c7-5656-490b-ac11-dd1d07d52b52-97eee867.avif "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/2a8d68c7-5656-490b-ac11-dd1d07d52b52.avif")

## S04 feature-callout

PRECISION EDITING
### Precise, multi-turn editing that holds
Editing is where GPT 2.5 makes its biggest leap. Change a single element - a product, a background, a line of text - and the rest of the frame holds. Multi-turn stability means earlier edits survive later ones instead of the image degrading over a long conversation, so you can refine step by step with confidence. It accepts up to 16 reference images on an edit to steer the result.
![image creative 480x480: GPT Image 2.5 precise multi-turn editing](media/9a903c68-e66a-4b63-ae4a-ebab3398abea-16bf8d36.avif "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/9a903c68-e66a-4b63-ae4a-ebab3398abea.avif")

## S05 feature-callout

FASTER GENERATION
### Two variants: Flare and Sunburst
GPT 2.5 comes in two variants: Flare, tuned for speed at two to four times the pace of GPT Image 2, and Sunburst, tuned for precision - on this page, Start generating opens Flare, and you can switch to Sunburst anytime in the AI Playground. Overall, generation latency is up to 50% lower than Images 2.0 - and sketch-based generation, templates, and shared prompts give you more control over how each image comes together.
![image creative 480x480: GPT Image 2.5 Flare and Sunburst variants](media/bd1f5ad3-985d-44dc-80db-0eea0cca187d-7b954d64.avif "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/bd1f5ad3-985d-44dc-80db-0eea0cca187d.avif")
