## S05 feature-callout

### Get familiar with GPT Image 1.5 and Midjourney
GPT Image 1.5 focuses on precision and control. It follows complex prompts closely, renders text accurately, and produces clean, structured outputs. Midjourney focuses on artistic expression, generating visually rich images with strong composition, lighting, and stylistic depth.

## S06 feature-callout

### Explore image quality: precision vs artistic mastery
The difference comes down to intent. GPT Image 1.5 produces accurate, structured visuals with reliable text and layout control, making it ideal for commercial and brand-focused work. Midjourney V7 delivers more expressive images with cinematic lighting, rich textures, and a distinct artistic style.

### See how accuracy and artistic style differ
Creative workflows differ significantly. GPT Image 1.5 offers precise control through structured prompts and conversational editing, making it predictable and repeatable. Midjourney emphasizes exploration, with personalization, style references, and remix tools that shape outputs based on creative direction.

## S08 feature-callout

### Review speed, pricing, and access
Speed varies depending on mode. GPT Image 1.5 delivers consistent generation times, while Midjourney can be slower unless using Draft Mode. Pricing also differs - GPT uses pay-per-image, while Midjourney requires a subscription. GPT Image offers API access, while Midjourney remains a closed platform.
![image creative 480x480: GPT Image vs Midjourney speed pricing and access](media/8500247f-49da-48c3-9327-3ded7fe9fb67-607bea63.webp "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/8500247f-49da-48c3-9327-3ded7fe9fb67.webp")

## S09 feature-callout

### Find your fit: GPT Image 1.5 or Midjourney V7
If you are aiming for precise, repeatable outputs with strong text rendering and prompt control, GPT Image 1.5 is the better fit, especially for designs with text, brand assets, and structured layouts. If you want expressive visuals with artistic depth and creative flexibility, Midjourney V7 is a stronger choice, ideal for concept art, editorial imagery, and creative exploration. If you want both, use Picsart to compare outputs in one place.
