## S03 feature-callout

MEET THE MODELS
### Get familiar with Recraft V4 Styles and GPT Image 2
Both Recraft V4 Styles and GPT Image 2 generate images from a prompt, but they are built for different jobs. Recraft V4 Styles is a style-consistency specialist - lock a reusable look from 1 to 10 references and hold it across every generation. GPT Image 2 is OpenAI's photorealism-and-text flagship - native 4K output with roughly 99% text accuracy and studio-grade realism. Try them both in our AI Playground .

## S04 feature-callout

STYLE VS PHOTOREALISM
### Style consistency vs photoreal output
Recraft V4 Styles exists to keep a look consistent - it captures texture, composition, and rendering from your references and reproduces them across a whole set, which is why its Precise mode was preferred 91.6% of the time for style match in blind testing. GPT Image 2 optimizes for a different axis: true-to-life photorealism with natural color science and lighting. Reach for Recraft to stay on-brand across many images; reach for GPT Image 2 when you need one photoreal hero shot.
![image creative 480x480: ](media/4e20e485-d98c-4465-99f6-3aa49530f974-5d758709.avif "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/4e20e485-d98c-4465-99f6-3aa49530f974.avif")

## S05 feature-callout

TEXT RENDERING
### Readable text: which renders type better
GPT Image 2 is built to treat text as content, not decoration - roughly 99% character-level accuracy across Latin, CJK, Hindi, and Bengali, so posters, packaging, and UI mockups come out with legible headlines and fine print. Recraft V4 Styles renders clean, on-style text within a locked look. If typography accuracy and multilingual signage are the priority, GPT Image 2 leads; if that text needs to match a consistent brand style across a set, Recraft keeps it on-look.
![image creative 480x480: ](media/57459daf-abda-41cf-96e7-508b2214aaff-a1e19627.avif "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/57459daf-abda-41cf-96e7-508b2214aaff.avif")

## S06 feature-callout

REFERENCES & CONTROL
### References, style locking, and control
Recraft V4 Styles takes 1 to 10 references from anywhere - other generators, brand guidelines, or existing assets - with no training, and offers Precise or Flexible match modes to dial how closely it follows them. GPT Image 2 works from prompts and image inputs with autoregressive reasoning that plans structure before it renders, and returns up to eight images per prompt. Recraft gives you style-lock control; GPT Image 2 gives you photoreal range and volume.
