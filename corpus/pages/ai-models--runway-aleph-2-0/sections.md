## S03 feature-callout

### What is Runway Aleph?
Runway Aleph is an in-context AI video model - the first of its kind to truly understand existing footage and rewrite it from natural-language prompts. Instead of generating video from scratch, Runway Aleph edits real clips: it changes camera angles, transforms lighting and scenes, isolates subjects, and swaps objects, all while preserving the original look, motion, and continuity. Aleph 2.0 is the next-generation release, launching in Picsart end of May 2026.

### Edit real footage with Runway Aleph
Runway Aleph 2.0 turns any clip into a creative canvas. ## S07 feature-callout

### Transform lighting and scenes with Runway Aleph
Runway Aleph 2.0 rewrites the look of any clip - turn morning into golden hour, summer into winter, or studio into street. Lighting adjusts across the whole frame: shadows reorient, highlights warm or cool, and reflections update so the new look feels shot, not filtered.
