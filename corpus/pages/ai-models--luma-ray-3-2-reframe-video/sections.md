## S04 feature-callout

### What is Luma Ray 3.2 Reframe?
Luma Ray 3.2 is Luma Labs’ latest AI video model, built for precision with frame-level multi-keyframe control and a native HDR pipeline. Reframe is its standout editing capability: it reshapes an existing shot into a new aspect ratio, intelligently filling the added space and extending the frame while preserving the original lighting and motion. The result is photorealistic video you can adapt to any format without losing cinematic quality.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/0a44cf69-38ff-4ab8-8c41-840b66310f74-741e6f2b.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/0a44cf69-38ff-4ab8-8c41-840b66310f74.webm")

## S05 feature-callout

### Luma Ray 3.2 Reframe capabilities
Reframe adapts video across every common aspect ratio — 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, 9:16, 21:9, and 9:21 — expanding the frame and generating new content that matches the original scene. Powered by Ray 3.2, it pairs this with frame-level multi-keyframe direction, native HDR with EXR-ready output, and up to 1080p generation. The model keeps reflections, shadows, and materials consistent as it fills new space, so reframed footage stays believable and production-ready.
