## S04 feature-callout

### What is Pika Frames?
Pika Frames is the keyframe transition feature from Pika Labs, part of the Pika 2.2 model. It lets creators upload start and end images as visual anchor points, and the AI generates smooth, natural motion between them. Supporting up to 5 keyframes per sequence with customizable transition durations from 1 to 10 seconds each, Pika Frames gives creators deterministic control over AI video output that pure text-to-video models can’t provide. It supports 7 aspect ratios covering every major platform format.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/0f8df66b-1791-43f0-aaa2-5e3c92b8e593-4b929787.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/0f8df66b-1791-43f0-aaa2-5e3c92b8e593.webm")

## S05 feature-callout

### Pika Frames capabilities
Pika Frames excels at giving creators frame-level control over AI video generation. Define up to 5 keyframes as visual anchor points and the model interpolates smooth, natural motion between them with customizable transition timing. It supports loop animations for seamless infinite cycles, PikaScenes for composing shots from separate character and environment references, and 7 aspect ratios from landscape to portrait covering every social platform format.

## S08 feature-callout

### Why creators choose Pika Frames
Pika Frames gives creators something no other AI video model offers: deterministic keyframe control over generated motion. Instead of hoping a text prompt produces the right movement, creators define exactly where the video starts and ends. This makes it ideal for product animations, morphing effects, and precise creative transitions. Integrated into Picsart’s AI Video Generator, it brings frame-level creative control to every creator’s workflow.
