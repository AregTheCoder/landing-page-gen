## S04 feature-callout

### What is Veo 3.1 Fast?
Veo 3.1 Fast is the speed-optimized variant of Google DeepMind’s Veo 3.1 video generation model, built to deliver the same cinematic quality at 30% faster generation speed and one-third the cost. It supports native 4K resolution up to 3840×2160, generates synchronized audio with natural dialogue and sound effects, and maintains strong character consistency across scenes. Veo 3.1 Fast is designed for creators and production teams who need high-quality output on accelerated timelines.
