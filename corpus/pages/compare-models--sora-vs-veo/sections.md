## S04 feature-callout

### Get familiar with Sora 2 and Veo 3.1
Sora 2 is built for storytelling. It handles long-form narrative structure, maintains consistency across scenes, and follows detailed prompts with impressive accuracy. Veo 3.1 focuses on cinematic output, combining native 4K resolution with synchronized audio, realistic lighting, and strong dialogue generation for polished video production.

## S05 feature-callout

### Explore visual quality, audio, and output differences
Resolution and audio create the biggest differences in this Sora vs Veo comparison. Veo 3.1 generates native 4K video at selectable frame rates with fully layered stereo audio, making it especially strong for cinematic delivery and YouTube content. Sora 2 generates at lower native resolution but excels at narrative continuity and dialogue-heavy scenes. Both models generate audio natively, but their strengths differ. Veo produces richer cinematic soundscapes with music and ambient depth, while Sora focuses on synchronized dialogue and scene coherence across longer sequences.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/5f89a61f-5b17-4c57-a880-3bf3798ca8a7-e8b05600.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/5f89a61f-5b17-4c57-a880-3bf3798ca8a7.webm")

## S06 feature-callout

### Understand storytelling, characters, and camera control
Sora 2 stands out for narrative depth. It maintains character consistency across multiple scenes and handles complex prompts with minimal drift, making it ideal for scripted storytelling and branded narrative content. Veo 3.1 takes a more cinematic approach. Features like Ingredients to Video and Scene Extension support longer visual continuity, while native vertical output and Google ecosystem integration make it particularly effective for social and creator workflows. In short, Sora prioritizes narrative structure, while Veo prioritizes cinematic polish.

## S07 feature-callout

### Review speed, pricing, and ecosystem access
Both models support faster generation modes for rapid iteration, though they optimize differently. Sora emphasizes prompt fidelity and coherence, while Veo’s Fast variant prioritizes throughput and rendering speed. Pricing also differs. Sora 2 is bundled with ChatGPT Plus, while Veo 3.1 starts at a lower entry price through Google AI plans.
