## S11 faq

### Sepia filter FAQ
#### What is a sepia filter?
A sepia color filter is a tool that changes the color and contrast of an image by allowing more brown and tan tones to pass through the filter while blocking most other colors. The end result is a photo that contains varying degrees of warm brown hues, which contrast with one another. Sepia effects are used to purposely subdue the original colors of the image to create the appearance of aging.
