## S03 feature-callout

MEET THE MODELS
### Get to know MiniMax Music 3 and ElevenLabs Music v2
Both MiniMax Music 3 and ElevenLabs Music v2 turn a plain-language prompt into a finished track with vocals or instrumentals - but they lead with different strengths. MiniMax Music 3 hands you a dedicated lyrics field and configurable audio encoding, so the song says your exact words and exports clean. ElevenLabs Music v2 leans into length control, generating anything from a 10-second hook to a 10-minute score. Try both in Picsart's AI Playground .

## S04 feature-callout

LYRICS & VOCALS
### Bring your own lyrics, or describe the vibe
This is the clearest split between the two. MiniMax Music 3 has a dedicated lyrics field - paste your words and it performs them line for line, with an optional lyric optimizer to tighten the phrasing. ElevenLabs Music v2 shapes vocals from your text prompt instead, which is fast when you want a vibe rather than specific words. If the song has to say exactly what you wrote, MiniMax leads; if you're chasing a sound, ElevenLabs keeps it simple.

## S05 feature-callout

LENGTH & FORMAT
### Track length vs export control
ElevenLabs Music v2 lets you set an exact duration - anywhere from a 10-second sting to a full 10-minute piece - which is ideal for scoring long edits or podcasts. MiniMax Music 3 focuses on the file instead: configurable audio encoding lets you pick MP3, WAV, or PCM and dial in sample rate and bitrate, so tracks drop into your project export-ready. Pick ElevenLabs when length matters most; pick MiniMax when output format and quality do.

## S06 feature-callout

INSTRUMENTALS EITHER WAY
### Vocals or pure instrumentals, from both
Need backing music with no vocals? Both models have an instrumental mode - flip one toggle and you get clean beds, loops, and score without a vocal line. MiniMax Music 3 pairs that with its encoding controls for export-ready stems, while ElevenLabs Music v2 pairs it with flexible length for full-scene instrumentals. Either way, you're one setting away from a vocal-free track.
