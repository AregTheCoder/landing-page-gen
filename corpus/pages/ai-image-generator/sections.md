## S15 faq

### Frequently asked questions
01
#### What can I do with a text-to-image AI generator?
A text-to-image AI generator turns written descriptions into images. Type a prompt describing your subject, style, and mood, and the AI produces a matching visual. With 48 style filters and 100 presets, you can create everything from photorealistic product shots to stylized illustrations and social media graphics.
02
#### How does AI generate images?
AI image generation uses neural networks trained on large visual datasets. When you enter a text prompt, the model interprets your description and builds an image through processes like diffusion. Different models use different architectures, which is why outputs vary - and why having 30 models in one platform gives you more creative range.
03
#### Is my AI-generated image going to be completely unique?
Yes. Each AI generated image is created fresh from your specific prompt, model selection, and style filter combination. Even identical prompts produce variations across different runs and models. No two outputs are exactly the same.
04
#### Can I use AI images for personal and commercial use?
Usage rights depend on the specific AI model and your Picsart plan. Many AI generated images can be used for personal projects, social media, and commercial purposes. Check the licensing details for each model within the platform to confirm eligibility for your specific output.
05
#### Is Picsart's AI Image Generator free?
Yes. Picsart offers a free AI image generator with limited generations per day. You can create text-to-image AI visuals across multiple models and styles at no cost. Premium plans unlock higher generation limits, priority processing, and access to additional models.
