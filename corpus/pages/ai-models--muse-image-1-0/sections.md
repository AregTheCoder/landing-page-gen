## S03 feature-callout

META SUPERINTELLIGENCE LABS
### What is Muse Image 1.0?
Muse Image 1.0 (also called Muse Image) is the first image model from Meta Superintelligence Labs. What sets it apart is that it's agentic: rather than turning a prompt straight into an image, it reasons about what you asked for, gathers real-world context, and plans the result before generating. That extra thinking shows - Muse Image holds the No. 2 spot on the LMArena leaderboard for text-to-image, single-image editing, and multi-image editing by human preference.

## S04 feature-callout

REASONS, THEN RENDERS
### It thinks and searches before it generates
Muse Image 1.0 pairs with Meta's Muse Spark language model to work like an agent. It interprets your prompt, pulls real-time context from the web, searches for reference imagery, plans the layout and composition, and even self-refines - invoking search and reasoning tools to get details right before a single pixel is drawn. You can dial reasoning strength between low and high, so quick ideas stay fast while complex, real-world-accurate scenes get the full treatment.

## S06 feature-callout

MULTI-IMAGE INPUT
### Blend multiple reference photos into one image
Muse Image 1.0 is built to compose, not just generate. Bring several reference photos - a subject, a setting, a style - and it reasons about how they fit together, then blends them into a single high-quality image. Generate 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, or 10 options per request across aspect ratios from square 1:1 to wide 16:9 and tall 9:16, and export as PNG, JPEG, or WebP. It's image generation that understands your inputs instead of overwriting them.
