## S11 feature-callout

### Write the perfect prompt for AI-generated tattoos
The more detailed your prompts, the better the results. Instead of a generic prompt like "Generate a black and white tattoo of an owl," narrow things down for better results. "Generate a black and white tattoo design with an owl and a full moon in a geometric style" will get you closer to a more realistic tattoo design. Take your time with prompts, and experiment with different variants to get closer to your final vision.
