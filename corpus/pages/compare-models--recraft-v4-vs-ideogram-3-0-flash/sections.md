## S04 feature-callout

### Get familiar with Recraft V4 and Ideogram 3.0 Flash
Recraft V4 focuses on design intelligence. It understands layout, spacing, and visual hierarchy, producing outputs that resemble professional design work rather than simple prompt outputs. Ideogram 3.0 Flash focuses on typography and speed, generating highly readable text inside images while supporting rapid iteration for design-heavy workflows.
![image creative 480x480: Recraft V4 and Ideogram 3.0 Flash overview](media/e59fddfe-5cd7-43c8-bc32-37979d155d43-5c552f94.avif "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/e59fddfe-5cd7-43c8-bc32-37979d155d43.avif")

## S05 feature-callout

### Explore image quality: design intelligence vs typography precision
The difference becomes clear in how each model handles text and composition. Ideogram 3.0 Flash delivers the most accurate text rendering available, producing crisp typography across complex layouts such as posters, signage, and packaging. Recraft V4, however, integrates text more naturally into the overall design, balancing hierarchy, spacing, and visual flow. When text accuracy is critical, Ideogram leads. When visual composition and brand consistency matter, Recraft stands out.
![image creative 480x480: Recraft V4 vs Ideogram 3.0 Flash image quality and typography](media/666b9bad-6571-40a2-9ff2-c4b3ab1cc8fa-9460bce2.avif "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/666b9bad-6571-40a2-9ff2-c4b3ab1cc8fa.avif")

## S06 feature-callout

### See how creative tools and workflows differ
Creative workflows are built around different strengths. Recraft V4 introduces Exploration Mode, which generates multiple visual directions from a single prompt, helping designers discover styles without rewriting prompts. It also supports native SVG output, making it uniquely suited for scalable design assets like logos and icons. Ideogram 3.0 Flash focuses on speed and accessibility. Its Magic Prompt expands simple inputs into richer descriptions, while built-in style presets and fast generation make it ideal for quick iteration and high-volume design workflows.
![image creative 480x480: Recraft V4 Exploration Mode vs Ideogram 3.0 Flash Magic Prompt](media/4dc25da3-0f5d-4bf8-b39e-91d3af6f0293-9c12ab46.avif "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/4dc25da3-0f5d-4bf8-b39e-91d3af6f0293.avif")

## S07 feature-callout

### Review pricing, credits, and access
Pricing is one of the clearest differences. On Picsart, Ideogram 3.0 Flash costs 1 credit per generation, while Recraft V4 costs 2 credits. This makes Ideogram more cost-efficient for high-volume workflows, especially when generating many variations. However, Recraft offers capabilities Ideogram cannot match, particularly SVG vector output and advanced design composition. Both models are available in the same environment, so you can switch between them without managing separate tools or subscriptions.
![image creative 480x480: Recraft V4 vs Ideogram 3.0 Flash pricing and access](media/5fb01b1e-3859-49ae-9616-ef1fb20547a2-d6e2c495.avif "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/5fb01b1e-3859-49ae-9616-ef1fb20547a2.avif")

## S08 feature-callout

### Match your project with the right model
Choose Recraft V4 when design quality and brand consistency matter, especially for logos, brand assets, editorial layouts, and scalable vector graphics. Choose Ideogram 3.0 Flash when text accuracy and speed matter most, especially for posters, packaging, social media graphics, and typography-heavy designs.
