## S14 faq

### Frequently asked questions
01
#### What is Picsart Storyline?
Picsart Storyline is a tool that turns text prompts into structured narratives automatically. This tool goes further by combining script writing, AI voiceover generation, and animated scene creation into one connected storytelling workflow.
02
#### Can AI generate animated stories?
Yes. An AI story generator can create fully animated story videos by transforming scripts into scene-based visuals, adding characters, and applying synchronized voiceover automatically.
03
#### Does Picsart Storyline create cartoon characters?
Yes. Picsart Storyline includes an AI character generator and a cartoon character generator that lets you create consistent, story-driven characters for animated scenes.
04
#### Can I download the script only?
Yes. You can export the generated script separately without downloading the animation or voiceover, thanks to modular output options.
05
#### Can I download voiceover only?
Yes. The built-in AI voiceover generator allows you to download narration audio independently from the script or animated video.
