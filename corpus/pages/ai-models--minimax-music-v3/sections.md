## S03 feature-callout

MEET MINIMAX MUSIC v3
### Write and perform full songs from a prompt
MiniMax Music v3 writes, arranges, and performs complete songs from a single plain-language prompt. Describe a genre, mood, or scene and it builds the whole track — melody, instrumentation, and vocals — or strips it back to a purely instrumental cut when you just need a backing bed. Bring your own lyrics and it sings them word for word; leave them out and it writes them for you. Configurable audio encoding lets you set the exact format and quality, so every track lands ready to use.
![image creative 480x480: What is MiniMax Music v3](media/3abac639-c551-4f79-b0a9-613f51cafcc0-b6b02f16.avif "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/3abac639-c551-4f79-b0a9-613f51cafcc0.avif")

## S04 feature-callout

VOCALS, INSTRUMENTALS, YOUR CALL
### Vocals, instrumentals, or both — you decide
Direct the whole track, not just a loop. Call for vocals or go fully instrumental, set the genre, tempo, and mood, and hand it your lyrics to perform word for word. Configurable audio encoding lets you lock the output format, sample rate, and bitrate, so tracks export clean the first time. From pop and hip-hop to cinematic and ambient, MiniMax Music v3 covers the range you need for original songs, soundtracks, and background audio.
