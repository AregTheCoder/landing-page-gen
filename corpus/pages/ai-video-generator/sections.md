## S13 faq

### AI Video Generator FAQ
01
#### What is an AI Video Generator?
An AI Video Generator turns text prompts or images into videos automatically. You can create videos without filming, editing, or using complex software. Tools like Sora, Veo 3.1, Runway, Kling V3, Seedance, Pika, and Luma handle everything, from visuals to motion and audio, in one place.
02
#### How does text to video work?
Text to video works by turning your prompt into a video sequence. Describe the style, mood, action, and setting, and the AI generates visuals to match. The more detailed your prompt, the more accurate and refined your video output will be.
03
#### How does image-to-video work?
Image to video works by uploading an image and applying motion or style prompts. The AI animates your image into a dynamic video with transitions, movement, and effects, turning static visuals into engaging clips ready for sharing.
04
#### Which AI models are available?
You can choose from leading AI models, including Veo 3.1 (audio sync), Kling V3 (social content, lip-sync), Runway (cinematic styles), Seedance 1 Pro and Seedance 2 (motion and dance), Pika (frame-level styling), Luma (smooth transitions), and Sora and Wan for advanced generation.
05
#### Can I customize the video output?
Yes, you can customize your AI video by adjusting duration, tone, pacing, visual style, motion, and transitions. You can also refine prompts and regenerate variations to explore different creative directions quickly, without using a traditional editing timeline.
