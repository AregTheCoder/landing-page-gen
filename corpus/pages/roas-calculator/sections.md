### What is ROAS and why it matters
ROAS or Return on Ad Spend, measures how much revenue you earn for every dollar spent on advertising. The ROAS formula is simple: Revenue ÷ Ad Spend. Unlike ROI, ROAS focuses solely on ad performance. Knowing how ROAS is calculated helps optimize campaigns across platforms like Google Ads, Facebook Ads, e-commerce and creative tools like Picsart’s Ad Maker .

### How to calculate ROAS
To calculate ROAS, use the formula: (Revenue from advertising ÷ Cost of advertising) × 100. This tells you how much revenue you earn for every dollar spent. For example, if you spent $1,000 on Facebook ads and made $3,000 in revenue, your ROAS would be ($3,000 ÷ $1,000) × 100 = 300%. Understanding the ROAS calculation formula is key to evaluating ad performance. The built-in Picsart ROAS Calculator helps you get accurate results fast, so you can focus on campaign strategy without manual number crunching.

## S07 feature-callout

### Breakeven ROAS Calculator for smarter budgeting
Break-even ROAS is the return needed to cover your ad costs. It’s calculated with the formula: 1 ÷ Profit Margin. For example, with a 25% profit margin, your break-even ROAS is 1 ÷ 0.25 = 4.0. Picsart’s breakeven ROAS Calculator helps determine this threshold instantly, ensuring campaigns remain profitable from the start.
