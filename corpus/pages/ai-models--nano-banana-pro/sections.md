## S04 feature-callout

### What is Nano Banana Pro?
Nano Banana Pro is Google DeepMind’s image generation and editing model built on Gemini 3 Pro Image. It is designed for situations where detail, clarity, and control matter. It delivers sharper textures, more natural lighting, and stronger composition overall. It also handles text far better than the original Nano Banana, which makes a noticeable difference for branded content, signage, or anything typography-heavy. ## S07 feature-callout

### Why creators choose Nano Banana Pro
Nano Banana Pro delivers the detail and control creators need for professional-quality output. It produces sharper textures, more natural lighting, and stronger composition than standard models - with advanced text rendering that handles multilingual typography, signage, and branded content. With support for up to 4K resolution, subject consistency across up to 5 people, and the ability to blend up to 14 images, it’s built for serious creative work.
