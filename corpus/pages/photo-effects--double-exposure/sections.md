It started as an accident. Back in the 1800s, photographers noticed ghost-like images on their film. Instead of trying to fix it, some embraced it. And a new artform was born. Today, you see Double Exposure all over Instagram. It lends a dreamy mood to photos. It tells a story. And with Picsart, it’s easy to make.But what is double exposure exactly? In simple terms, double exposure is the act of combining two photos into a single image, creating something truly unique. It may sound like it should be complicated, but the process — also called multiple exposure — is actually really easy. It’s so simple in fact, you just add a photo, layer on another, and tinker with Picsart photo editing tools to make the magic come to life .
