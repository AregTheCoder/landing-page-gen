## S03 feature-callout

IMAGE IN, CAMERA OUT
### How MiniMax H3 Max Camera Controls works
Start from a still image and hand the model a camera trajectory instead of relying on a text prompt alone. MiniMax H3 Max Camera Controls reads 2 to 12 keyframes - each one a point in time with an azimuth, elevation, and distance - and interpolates a smooth path between them, so the shot travels exactly the way you plotted it. Add a prompt to animate the scene as well, or leave it out to keep everything still but the lens.
![video ui-screenshot 480x480: ](media/81082e58-eac5-4e3b-86dd-09d5622e7c38-6d72f1bb.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/81082e58-eac5-4e3b-86dd-09d5622e7c38.webm")

## S04 feature-callout

AZIMUTH, ELEVATION, DISTANCE
### Plot the camera path keyframe by keyframe
Every keyframe sets three things: azimuth to swing the camera around the subject, elevation to raise or lower the angle, and distance to move closer or pull away. Chain up to 12 of them and MiniMax H3 Max Camera Controls builds the in-between motion - a slow orbit, a rising crane, a sharp punch-in - with frame-accurate timing. It's the kind of camera choreography that usually needs a rig and a dolly, driven from a single photo.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/555fd7e1-5d0e-4873-abca-eb76cdfc2f3b-9a04c225.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/555fd7e1-5d0e-4873-abca-eb76cdfc2f3b.webm")

## S05 feature-callout

PROMPT OPTIONAL
### Freeze the scene, move only the camera
Leave the prompt blank and MiniMax H3 Max Camera Controls holds your scene perfectly still while the camera travels through it - a clean parallax glide across a product, a portrait, or a landscape with nothing else in motion. Want life in the frame too? Add a prompt and set prompt expansion to balanced or quality to decide how much the model elaborates, and drop in a seed whenever you need the exact same move again.
