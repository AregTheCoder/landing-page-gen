## S11 feature-list

Still got questions? Find answers
[Email us](https://picsart.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#fe8d8b8e8e918c8abe8e979d8d9f8c8ad09d9193)
01
How does the AI learn my brand style?
The AI analyzes your past creative assets—like ads, colors, copy, and visuals - to detect consistent patterns in tone, imagery and layout. It then uses this data to generate new content that matches your brand’s look and feel.
02
Which ad platforms does AI Ad Maker support for direct publishing?
AI Ad Maker supports direct publishing to Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google and TikTok. You can connect your accounts once and push campaigns live without leaving the platform.
03
How does the multi-platform publishing work?
After generating your creative, AI Ad Maker formats it for each platform’s specs. You can review, customize and publish to multiple ad channels from a single workflow - no manual resizing or exporting needed.
04
Can multiple team members work on the same campaign?
Yes. AI Ad Maker allows collaborative editing, commenting and approval workflows, so teams can build and launch campaigns together in real time.
05
What kind of competitor data can I access?
You can view top-performing ads from competitors, track industry benchmarks and uncover content trends to inform your creative and media strategy - all in one place.
