## S08 faq

### Video Studio FAQ
01
#### Is Picsart Video Studio free?
Yes. You can trim, merge, resize, compress, convert, and edit video for free in your browser - no signup or installation needed.
02
#### Do I need to install anything?
No. Video Studio runs entirely in your web browser, so there is nothing to download or install.
03
#### Can I remove the background from a video?
Yes. The Background tool removes or replaces a video's background right in your browser - no green screen needed.
04
#### What video formats can I use?
You can import common formats like MP4, MOV, and WebM, and export in your chosen format and resolution.
05
#### Can I blur faces in a video?
Yes. The Face Blur tool detects faces and lets you blur, pixelate, or apply a glitch effect to protect privacy.
