## S03 feature-callout

MEET THE MODELS
### Get familiar with Flux 3 and Sora 2
Both Flux 3 and Sora 2 generate cinematic video with synchronized native audio, but with different scope. Flux 3 is Black Forest Labs' first fully multimodal model - image, video up to 20 seconds, and native audio from a single prompt in one pass. Sora 2 is OpenAI's flagship video model, built for physically accurate motion and real-world subject injection. Try them both in our AI Playground .

## S04 feature-callout

LENGTH, AUDIO & RESOLUTION
### Compare clip length, audio, and resolution
Both models reach up to 20 seconds with synchronized native audio - Flux 3 generates sound in the same pass as the video, and Sora 2 delivers synced dialogue and sound effects. On resolution, Sora 2 outputs up to 1080p at 24 or 30fps, while Flux 3 renders high-definition video as part of a single multimodal generation. If you want a defined resolution spec for cinematic clips, Sora 2 is clear; if you want image, video, and audio together, Flux 3 leads.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/3dba0d37-fdb2-4038-a29f-0055a0007f2f-55cd40a5.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/3dba0d37-fdb2-4038-a29f-0055a0007f2f.webm")

## S05 feature-callout

MULTIMODAL VS CINEMATIC
### One-pass multimodality vs cinematic realism
Flux 3's headline is that picture, motion, and native audio come out together in a single pass - not stitched from separate tools - and it accepts text, audio, video, and up to 10 image references for multi-shot storyboarding. Sora 2 focuses on cinematic realism: physically accurate human movement, complex actions, and real-world injection that places real subjects into a scene with accurate appearance and voice.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/97c0eeeb-8b0a-4aae-9f9d-be2831ed62be-51cf6ce2.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/97c0eeeb-8b0a-4aae-9f9d-be2831ed62be.webm")

## S06 feature-callout

INPUTS & CONTROL
### References, inputs, and creative control
Flux 3 takes up to 10 image references plus text, audio, and video, enabling complex editing, remixing, and multi-shot storyboarding that pure text-to-video models can't handle. Sora 2 works from a text prompt and adds real-world injection, letting you drop real people into AI scenes. Flux 3 offers more input types and shot-level assembly; Sora 2 offers standout realism and subject injection.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/304e98ad-1680-48a1-8bac-df48f9190df5-eb9279b4.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/304e98ad-1680-48a1-8bac-df48f9190df5.webm")
