## S04 feature-callout

STYLE-LOCKED GENERATION
### Turn reference images into a locked, reusable style
Recraft V4 Styles Pro is the premium tier of Recraft's style-consistent model, built by the team behind CatBoost. Instead of describing a look in words, upload one to five reference images - and they can come from anywhere, not just Recraft - and the model captures the palette, texture, lighting, and composition they share, then applies that exact style to whatever you prompt. There's no training or fine-tuning: attach references and generate. The result is an art-directed set of raster images where every asset feels like it came from the same creative brief. Prefer the standard tier? Recraft V4 Styles shares the same controls at a lower price.

## S05 feature-callout

PRECISION STYLE CONTROL
### Pro-grade detail, with your style locked in
Give Recraft V4 Styles Pro up to five style references and choose how closely to follow them - Precise locks onto the reference meticulously, down to texture and rendering, while Flexible matches the overall vibe when you want room to explore. The Pro tier sharpens fine detail, improves anatomy, and pushes realism for complex scenes. Generate up to six variations at once and output across a full range of aspect ratios - from square 1:1 to wide 16:9 and tall 9:16 - so a single locked style scales across every placement. Need clean, scalable output instead? Switch to Recraft V4 Styles Pro Vector .
