## S04 feature-callout

### What is Luma Ray 3.2?
Luma Ray 3.2 is Luma Labs’ latest AI video model, built for precision and photorealism. It turns text prompts or a reference image into cinematic clips up to 20 seconds long at 1080p, with frame-level multi-keyframe control for directing motion and pacing. A native HDR pipeline with 16-bit EXR output keeps lighting, reflections, and materials physically accurate, making Ray 3.2 a production-grade model for generating believable, high-fidelity video.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/0a44cf69-38ff-4ab8-8c41-840b66310f74-741e6f2b.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/0a44cf69-38ff-4ab8-8c41-840b66310f74.webm")

## S05 feature-callout

### Luma Ray 3.2 capabilities
Ray 3.2 pairs photorealistic generation with real directorial control. Place keyframes across a clip to choreograph motion and timing exactly, generate from text or an image, and render up to 1080p for clips as long as 20 seconds. Its native HDR pipeline produces 16-bit EXR-ready output for color grading and compositing, while expressive performance tracking preserves skeletal posture, gestures, and facial nuance for multiple subjects at once — so complex, human scenes stay convincing frame by frame.
