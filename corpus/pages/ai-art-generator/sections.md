### Frequently asked questions
01
#### What is AI-generated art?
AI-generated art is artwork created using artificial intelligence based on text prompts or reference images. It uses machine learning to turn ideas into visuals across different styles, techniques, and artistic movements, helping you quickly create unique and original pieces.
02
#### Is Picsart's AI Art Generator free to use?
You can start using Picsart's AI Art Generator for free with limited generations. To unlock more generations, higher resolution outputs, and access to additional styles and models, you can upgrade to a paid plan.
03
#### How do I create AI-generated art?
To create AI art, open the generator, type your idea, and choose a style or upload a reference image. Generate multiple variations, then refine your result with prompt edits or editing tools until it matches your vision.
04
#### What type of images can the AI Art Generator create?
You can create a wide range of visuals, including abstract art, illustrations, concept art, social media graphics, and print-ready designs. With 48+ styles and multiple AI models, you can explore different artistic directions from a single prompt.
05
#### Can I customize the AI-generated artwork?
Yes, you can refine your artwork using prompt-based edits or enhance it in the editor. Adjust colors, remove backgrounds, replace elements, or layer edits to fully customize your final image.
