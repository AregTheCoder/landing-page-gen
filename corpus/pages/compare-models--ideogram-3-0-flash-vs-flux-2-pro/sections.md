## S04 feature-callout

### Get familiar with Ideogram 3.0 Flash and Flux 2 Pro
Ideogram 3.0 Flash focuses on typography and design. It produces clean, readable text inside images and supports structured layouts for logos, posters, and brand visuals. Flux 2 Pro focuses on realism and performance, generating high-resolution images quickly with detailed lighting and textures.
![image creative 480x480: Ideogram 3.0 Flash and Flux 2 Pro AI image model overview](https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/f0619567-4a20-43d1-9932-029ce553d4f0.avif)

## S05 feature-callout

### Explore image quality: typography vs photorealism
Text is the biggest difference. Ideogram produces clear, consistent typography across complex layouts, making it ideal for designs with text. Flux 2 Pro handles text reasonably well but is less reliable. For visual realism, Flux takes the lead, generating more natural lighting, skin tones, and depth, making it better for editorial and photographic content.
![image creative 480x480: Ideogram 3.0 Flash typography vs Flux 2 Pro photorealism](https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/af02f9ab-66a0-481f-a8e5-a2732fd7a806.avif)

## S06 feature-callout

### Understand creative tools and workflow differences
Creative workflows differ across models. Ideogram provides built-in style presets and structured design control, making it easy to generate consistent layouts. Flux offers more flexibility with multi-reference inputs, fine-tuning, and precise color control. Ideogram is optimized for design workflows, while Flux supports more advanced customization.
![image creative 480x480: Ideogram 3.0 Flash vs Flux 2 Pro creative tools and workflow](https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/3323d3c0-89ae-4277-9816-ab80c2890074.avif)

## S07 feature-callout

### Review speed, pricing, and access
Speed favors Flux 2 Pro, generating images in seconds and supporting rapid iteration. Ideogram is slightly slower but still efficient for most workflows. Pricing depends on usage. Ideogram offers a free tier and subscription plans, while Flux uses pay-per-use pricing. Both are available on Picsart without separate subscriptions.
![image creative 480x480: Ideogram 3.0 Flash vs Flux 2 Pro speed pricing and access](https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/8ea9ba54-5daa-4b96-8cfe-69299d37f446.avif)

## S08 feature-callout

### Choose the right model for your use case
Choose Ideogram 3.0 Flash when text matters, especially for logos, posters, packaging, and brand assets that require clear typography. Choose Flux 2 Pro when visual quality matters most, with high-resolution, photorealistic output for editorial and product imagery.
![image creative 480x480: Choose Ideogram 3.0 Flash or Flux 2 Pro by use case](https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/a434e2d2-fb85-4e54-b0e1-416f4b5ce127.avif)
