## S04 feature-callout

### What is Seedance 1 Pro?
Seedance 1 Pro is ByteDance’s flagship AI video generation model and the top-ranked model on Artificial Analysis benchmarks for both text-to-video and image-to-video. It generates cinematic video with native multi-shot narrative capability, producing coherent sequences with seamless wide, medium, and close-up shot transitions in a single pass. The model supports diverse visual styles from photorealism to illustration, handles complex multi-agent interactions, and delivers precise camera movement control.

## S05 feature-callout

### Seedance 1 Pro capabilities
Seedance 1 Pro leads benchmarks in prompt adherence, motion quality, and visual aesthetics. Its standout feature is native multi-shot storytelling that generates coherent video with seamless transitions between wide, medium, and close-up shots while maintaining subject consistency throughout. The model handles complex scenes with multiple characters, precise camera movements, and diverse styles from photorealism to cyberpunk delivering up to 1080p output at 24fps in approximately 41 seconds per 5-second clip.
