### Why convert PSD to JPG?
JPG is the go-to format for publishing. It works across email, websites, CMS platforms, social media, and ads, while PSD files are large, layered, and tied to editing software. Exporting a PSD as JPG turns your design into a lightweight image that’s easy to share and load. When needed, you can also export designs to PSD to PNG and more formats for different use cases.
