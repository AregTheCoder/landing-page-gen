## S03 feature-callout

MEET THE MODELS
### Get familiar with Muse Image 1.0 and Nano Banana 2
Both Muse Image 1.0 and Nano Banana 2 turn a prompt into a high-quality image, but with different priorities. Muse Image 1.0 is Meta's agentic model - it reasons, searches the web, and blends your reference photos before it renders. Nano Banana 2 is Google's Flash-speed model, delivering near-Pro quality up to 4K in a fraction of the time. Generate with both in our AI Image Generator .
![image creative 480x480: Muse Image 1.0 and Nano Banana 2 overview](media/fac139f1-0aae-4888-99f2-8fdf603c8900-7568b314.webp "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/fac139f1-0aae-4888-99f2-8fdf603c8900.webp")

## S04 feature-callout

QUALITY & SPEED
### Compare quality, speed, and resolution
Nano Banana 2 is built for speed - Flash-fast generation up to 4K with up to 40% lower cost, ideal when you need volume. Muse Image 1.0 trades some speed for reasoning: it thinks through the prompt and can render places, products, and references with real-world accuracy. If raw throughput and resolution matter most, Nano Banana 2 leads; for reasoned, accurate scenes, Muse Image leads.
![image creative 480x480: Muse Image 1.0 vs Nano Banana 2 quality and speed](media/ea801baa-1c4c-45b9-85dc-8f4ceec012dc-530e1774.avif "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/ea801baa-1c4c-45b9-85dc-8f4ceec012dc.avif")

## S05 feature-callout

REASONING VS SPEED
### Agentic reasoning vs flash generation
Muse Image 1.0 works like an agent, pairing with Meta's Muse Spark model to pull real-time web context, search for reference imagery, and plan the composition before generating - with reasoning strength you can dial from low to high. Nano Banana 2 draws on Gemini's knowledge base for faithful prompt interpretation while keeping generation near-instant. One optimizes for accuracy, the other for speed.
![image creative 480x480: Muse Image 1.0 reasoning vs Nano Banana 2 speed](media/9e0a38de-ab11-4fdb-8871-1b223d100ab4-873f1e42.webp "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/9e0a38de-ab11-4fdb-8871-1b223d100ab4.webp?type=webp&to=min&r=1200&q=90")

## S06 feature-callout

REFERENCES & CONSISTENCY
### References, editing, and consistency
Muse Image 1.0 blends multiple reference photos into one cohesive image and ranks No. 2 on LMArena for single- and multi-image editing. Nano Banana 2 maintains up to five consistent characters and fourteen objects per scene with strong character consistency across complex compositions. Both accept your own images; Muse leans into reasoned blending, Nano Banana 2 into fast, consistent scaling.
![image creative 480x480: Muse Image 1.0 vs Nano Banana 2 references and consistency](media/b0bc3822-b65c-460b-974f-d4aa08dbd9d4-8c790899.avif "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/b0bc3822-b65c-460b-974f-d4aa08dbd9d4.avif")
