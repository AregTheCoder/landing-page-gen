## S03 feature-callout

MEET THE MODELS
### Get familiar with GPT Image 2.5 and Seedream 4.5
At a glance, both GPT Image 2.5 (GPT 2.5) and Seedream 4.5 turn a prompt into a high-quality image, but with different strengths. GPT 2.5 is OpenAI's precision model - sharp detail, ~99% text accuracy, and exact multi-turn editing. Seedream 4.5 is ByteDance's unified model that merges generation and editing in one workflow, with dense multilingual text and strong cost efficiency. ## S04 feature-callout

TEXT RENDERING
### Compare text accuracy and typography
Both models are text-rendering leaders. GPT Image 2.5 hits ~99% text accuracy for crisp English headlines and small type. Seedream 4.5 specializes in dense, multilingual text - including Chinese - across posters, packaging, and infographics with consistent formatting. For maximum English accuracy, GPT Image 2.5 leads; for dense multilingual layouts, Seedream 4.5 is exceptional.
![image creative 480x480: GPT Image 2.5 vs Seedream 4.5 text rendering](media/0b7a5efc-0346-47fc-b017-1ce46c610f35-b963ef56.webp "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/0b7a5efc-0346-47fc-b017-1ce46c610f35.webp")

## S05 feature-callout

EDITING & WORKFLOW
### Precise edits vs unified generation and editing
GPT Image 2.5 makes surgical single-element edits and stays stable across a long multi-turn session, with up to 16 references. Seedream 4.5 unifies generation and editing in one model - describe changes in plain language and reference up to 14 images to swap products, move text, or adjust lighting without leaving the workflow. GPT Image 2.5 favors precision; Seedream 4.5 favors an all-in-one flow.
![image creative 480x480: GPT Image 2.5 vs Seedream 4.5 editing workflow](media/cd966a96-6729-4bc1-bc45-6b0ff1adc81e-2e77cf49.webp "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/cd966a96-6729-4bc1-bc45-6b0ff1adc81e.webp")

## S06 feature-callout

RESOLUTION & COST
### Resolution ceilings and cost efficiency
GPT Image 2.5 outputs up to 4K (3840px max edge) with new xhigh and max quality tiers for extra headroom. Seedream 4.5 runs 1K-4K at standard output and up to 8K when you need extreme detail for print - all at roughly $0.04 per image, about 4x more cost-efficient than many competitors. For premium precision, GPT Image 2.5; for high-volume, high-res value, Seedream 4.5.
