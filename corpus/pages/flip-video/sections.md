## S05 feature-callout

### What does it mean to flip a video?
To flip video means to reverse its image direction, creating a mirror video effect. Flipping horizontally swaps the left and right sides, while flipping vertically turns the image upside down. Unlike rotating, flipping reflects the frame. If you want to create, trim, and enhance videos without professional skills or complex software, use Picsart’s Video Editor .

### Flip video horizontally or vertically
When you flip video horizontally, the left and right sides of the frame switch places - perfect for correcting mirrored selfie footage. If you flip video vertically, the top and bottom are reversed, creating an upside-down reflection effect.
