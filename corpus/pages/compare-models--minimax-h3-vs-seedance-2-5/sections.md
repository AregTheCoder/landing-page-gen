## S03 feature-callout

MEET THE MODELS
### Get familiar with MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.5
Both MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.5 turn a prompt into finished video with sound, but they optimize for different things. MiniMax H3 generates native 2K clips with synchronized audio baked in - sound-on video in a single pass, no post-production. Seedance 2.5 trades resolution for runway: one continuous 30-second take in 1080p with physics-accurate light and up to 50 references. Try them both in our AI Playground .

## S04 feature-callout

RESOLUTION, LENGTH, AUDIO
### Compare resolution, clip length, and audio
MiniMax H3 outputs native 2K at 24fps with native 32kHz stereo audio - the higher-resolution, sound-first option. Seedance 2.5 runs at 1080p but holds a full 30-second continuous take with dialogue in 20 languages and matched lip-sync. If you want maximum resolution and built-in sound for short clips, H3 leads; for the longest unbroken take with multilingual lip-sync, Seedance leads.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/88a278dc-8cfd-44e8-a4f1-bf6bb3b209e3-42634eb0.webm "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/88a278dc-8cfd-44e8-a4f1-bf6bb3b209e3.webm")

## S05 feature-callout

ONE PASS VS ONE TAKE
### Sound in one pass vs one unbroken take
MiniMax H3's edge is that audio is generated with the picture - dialogue, SFX, and ambient arrive synced, so a sound-on clip is finished in a single pass with no post-production. Seedance 2.5's edge is duration: a 30-second continuous shot with no cuts, so a character can enter, act, and land a line in one take. H3 is fastest to a finished sound-on clip; Seedance is best for long, uninterrupted scenes.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/6e7d98a6-2747-445b-ba57-8fcc815df18a-a800ecd1.webm "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/6e7d98a6-2747-445b-ba57-8fcc815df18a.webm")

## S06 feature-callout

CONSISTENCY & REFERENCES
### References and character consistency
MiniMax H3 uses omni-reference - up to 9 images, 3 videos, and 3 audio clips - to keep a character's look and voice on-model across shots. Seedance 2.5 pushes reference headroom further, locking face, wardrobe, and voice across up to 50 references for long, continuity-critical sequences. Both stay on-model; Seedance offers more reference capacity, while H3 references across audio and generates native sound.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/cf385074-fd5d-4c5c-b3df-e8406e000da2-7ccaa5c3.webm "https://pcdn.picsart.com/cms-uploads/cf385074-fd5d-4c5c-b3df-e8406e000da2.webm")
