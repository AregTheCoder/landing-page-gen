## S14 faq

### ComicMe: Frequently asked questions
01
#### What is ComicMe?
ComicMe is an AI comic book generator that turns your real photos into personalized, multi-page comic stories with illustrated panels, written dialogue, and genre-based narratives built around your characters.
02
#### How do I turn my photo into a comic book?
Upload your hero photo and optionally a villain, choose your genre, tone, and language, then generate. The AI creates a full comic book story in seconds.
03
#### Can I share my comic book with a link?
Yes. Once your comic book is generated, you get a unique shareable URL. Simply copy the link and send it to friends, post it on social media, or keep it saved to access your comic anytime.
04
#### What genres are available in the AI comic book generator?
You can choose from Superhero, Fantasy, Romance, Office, and more. Each genre creates a unique story arc tailored to your uploaded characters.
05
#### Can I add a villain to my comic book story?
Yes. Upload a second photo as the villain, and the AI will incorporate both characters into the same multi-page narrative.
