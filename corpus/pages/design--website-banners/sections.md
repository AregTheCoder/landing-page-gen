## S09 faq

### Website banner maker FAQ
#### What is a web banner?
A website banner is a clickable digital advertisement displayed on a website to drive traffic or promote something. Web banners are typically rectangular or square and placed at the top or side of a website, but you can also find them at the bottom of many websites. Although they’re traditionally a sales and advertising tool, website banners can be used to convey any information that’s meant to be viewed by as many site visitors as possible.
