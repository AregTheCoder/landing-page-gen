Picsart’s Infinite Frame tool is a visual sandbox where you can design without limits. By toggling beyond the standard frame, you unlock an infinite playground to explore, plan, and experiment. This visual workspace keeps ideas flowing and makes it easy to expand your creativity in any direction.
