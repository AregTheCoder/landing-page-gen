## S05 feature-callout

PRECISION CONTROLS
### Set your frames, then tune the prompt
MiniMax H3 Max gives you real control: set a start and end frame to lock how a shot begins and ends, then choose a prompt-expansion mode - disabled, balanced, or quality - to decide how much the model elaborates on your prompt. Add a seed for reproducible results, pick from 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, or 9:16, and set any length from 5 to 15 seconds.
