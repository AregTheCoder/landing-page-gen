## S04 feature-callout

### What is GPT Image 2?
GPT 2, also called ChatGPT Images 2.0, is OpenAI's latest image generation model, launched in April 2026. Built on a fundamentally new autoregressive architecture, it generates images 3-5x faster than its predecessor at significantly higher quality. Unlike diffusion-based models, GPT Image 2 leverages the full world knowledge of the GPT model family - understanding context, following complex multi-constraint prompts with 98% accuracy, and rendering text at near-perfect character-level precision. Native 2K resolution standard, 4K in beta, with flexible aspect ratios from 3:1 to 1:3.

## S06 feature-callout

### 99% text accuracy: typography that’s finally production-ready
Earlier AI image models treated text as decoration - blurry, misspelled, unusable. GPT Image 2 treats text as content. Posters with readable headlines and fine print, product packaging with accurate brand names and multilingual labels, UI mockups with real interface elements, infographics with legible data annotations - all generated in one pass. GPT 2 scored +316 points over GPT Image 1.5 in the Image Arena text rendering category, the largest single-category improvement ever recorded.
