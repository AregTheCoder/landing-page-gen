## S04 feature-callout

### Get familiar with Luma Ray 2 and Kling 3.0
Luma Ray 2 is built for realism. It generates video with natural lighting, accurate shadows, and cinematic depth of field, producing footage that closely resembles real-world camera output. Kling 3.0 is built for control, combining high-resolution output with multi-shot storyboarding and precise motion tools for structured video production.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/cd425145-8099-4846-81f0-1aab866aa8ac-3fd8fe2d.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/cd425145-8099-4846-81f0-1aab866aa8ac.webm")

## S05 feature-callout

### Explore video quality: photorealism vs 4K cinematic output
Resolution and realism define the difference. Kling 3.0 delivers native 4K video at up to 60fps, making it ideal for broadcast and high-resolution production. Luma Ray 2 outputs at 1080p but focuses on photorealistic fidelity, producing more natural lighting, depth, and texture detail. In a Luma vs Kling comparison, the choice depends on whether resolution or realism matters more.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/f584f8d2-9e46-4134-80c4-5805e11a79f9-0ffbcb05.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/f584f8d2-9e46-4134-80c4-5805e11a79f9.webm")

## S06 feature-callout

### Understand motion quality and visual realism
Motion behavior separates these models further. Luma Ray 2 produces highly natural movement, where objects behave with realistic weight, inertia, and environmental interaction. Camera motion feels organic, similar to real handheld or cinematic rigs. Kling 3.0 offers strong motion as well, but its strength lies in controlled movement through tools like trajectory guidance and Motion Brush. Luma focuses on realism; Kling focuses on direction.
![video creative 480x480: ](media/dcb3e2aa-2566-451a-b914-26ba0e3d3246-42c89179.webm "https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/dcb3e2aa-2566-451a-b914-26ba0e3d3246.webm")

## S07 feature-callout

### See how tools and control differ
Creative control differs significantly. Luma Ray 2 relies on natural generation, producing realistic results with minimal input and fast iteration. Kling 3.0 provides deeper control, allowing you to design multi-shot sequences, guide camera paths, and manipulate elements at a finer level. This makes Luma easier for realism-first workflows, while Kling is better for structured storytelling.

### Review speed, pricing, and access
Speed gives Luma Ray 2 an advantage, making it ideal for rapid experimentation and iteration. Kling 3.0 takes longer to generate, especially at 4K and with multi-shot scenes, but delivers higher-resolution output.
