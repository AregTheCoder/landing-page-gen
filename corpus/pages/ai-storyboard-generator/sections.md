## S10 feature-list

### Use the tools an AI storyboard needs
#### Text and script input
Start from a prompt, story outline, treatment, or script instead of drawing every frame by hand.
#### Scene breakdown
Turn the story into ordered beats with a clear purpose for every frame.
#### Character continuity
Carry the same cast, wardrobe, props, and locations across the full sequence.
#### Visual framing
Plan composition, camera distance, action, dialogue, and transitions before generation.
#### Format planning
Prepare scenes for vertical, landscape, or square video from the start.
#### Video handoff
Use approved frames to guide image generation, animation, editing, and final assembly.
