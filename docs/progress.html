Timeline

Foundation: the corpus

Sep 4-7 2026 · no paid calls

Before any generation: 199 live Picsart pages snapshotted and sectionized into a searchable corpus, 2,711 distinct assets measured from pixels alone (no vision API), and the measurements distilled into 15 style families - the reusable blocks that tell a worker exactly how each kind of Picsart image is built. Every later run briefs its workers from this corpus and is scored against it.

What it changed: Quality control needs a ground truth before the first credit is spent.

trial-3: one blind slot

Sep 7 2026 · 19 credits · 1 slots

A single randomly drawn slot, produced blind (the worker never sees the original), with an A/B between the pro image model and a video-model frame grab. The reviewer measured subject margins against the family rule and sent the record back for a rework round - the loop's first catch.

What it changed: Reviewers measure against family rules; a record can be fixed without re-rendering.

live-1: first full page

Sep 8 2026 · 50 credits · 11 slots

Every generated slot of a live tool page (hsl-color), all accepted. The post-run gap study found the originals carry a tool-panel overlay no family described - so the panel-overlay family was created. Press logos were pinned as never-generated, and a double-charging count parameter was caught and banned.

What it changed: Gap studies after each run grow the family system; third-party marks are never generated.

live-2: first parallel wave

Sep 8 2026 · 112 credits · 23 slots

A full 23-slot page produced by parallel workers in one wave, all accepted. The post-run audit found the blindness exclusions had silently failed - which produced blindcheck.py, a hard gate no worker spawns past since. The benchmark later scored this page at 38 flags: the baseline to beat.

What it changed: Blindness is now checked mechanically before any worker runs, not assumed.

live-3: benchmark re-run

Sep 9 2026 · 126 credits · 25 slots

The same page re-run under the improved system to measure progress: benchmark flags fell 38 to 25 while two more slots were generated, and composites gained real chrome drawn by lp-compose instead of asking the model to paint UI.

What it changed: Chrome (tiles, chips, pills) is drawn deterministically; models only make photographs.

trial-6: the device layer

Sep 9 2026 · 52 credits · 5 slots

Five slots whose originals demonstrate something (reference thumbnails in, styled output out) were re-run with a new device line and compose variants. The benchmark's four fit flags on those slots went to zero.

What it changed: A picture must demonstrate what the copy claims, not just look right.

live-4: composites at scale

Sep 10 2026 · 143 credits · 20 slots

A template/card page: 20 slots including the first worker-driven composite family (template-mockup), hero to gallery, all accepted after one rework wave on 8 under-filled tiles. Family match rose to 0.65. The review also exposed that gallery tiles had run on a cheaper model under a loophole - which led to the strict pro-model rule for every image.

What it changed: One model for everything visible; budget buys refinement depth, not cheaper pixels.

Restructure: Picsart Flow boards

Sep 10 2026 · no paid calls

Every workflow re-expressed in Picsart Flow's own model: a board of typed nodes from START to END, one engine and one model per node, validated by lp-flow check and rendered as a node sheet a person could rebuild on the Flow canvas. Gallery templates are permitted when they fit; blank board is the default. Slot caps doubled to fund refinement passes under the stricter model rule.

What it changed: The pipeline now speaks Picsart's own workflow language end to end.

showcase-1: 15 standalone images

Sep 10 2026 · 115 credits · 15 slots

First run on the board format and first with no source page: 15 independent images across 10 families. 13 delivered, zero rework rounds; workers rejected every loosely-matching gallery template on the three fits tests and recorded blank boards honestly. Gates caught seven concrete flaws (a rendered brand logo, legible signage, under-filled frames, series drift) and fixed each in one node. The two undelivered slots are blocked by an account storage quota, not by quality - both records carry exact resume points.

What it changed: The system now self-corrects inside a run: 7 catches, 0 review rework, ledger exact.

Standing controls

  • Blind by construction: workers never see the page's original assets; blindcheck.py blocks the wave until briefs and examples are provably clean.
  • Every paid call preflighted and priced before it runs; a hook denies wrong-connector, dry-run and over-cap calls and logs every asset URL to a ledger.
  • One model for every visible image (the pro image model); cheaper tiers only for composite thumbnails, and only when the copy names the model honestly.
  • Chrome is never generated: tiles, chips, pills, panels and labels are drawn deterministically by lp-compose from family templates.
  • Gates at every node: a step passes only on a named observation; 7 concrete catches in the latest run alone (brand logo, legible signage, series drift), each fixed in one node.
  • Workflows are Picsart Flow boards: validated wiring, honest blank-vs-template records, and a node sheet a person could rebuild on the Flow canvas.
  • Budget caps enforced by hook, 40 credits per slot / 600 per run; every run's balance delta has matched its ledger to the credit.
  • Every lesson lands in the playbook (prd.md) and every decision in the log (plan.md) the same day.
