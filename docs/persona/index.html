Frequently asked questions

01

What is a Picsart Persona?

Picsart Persona is a virtual influencer created using AI tools. It's a stylized digital character designed to publish content, build an audience, and represent a brand across social platforms.
02

How do I create an AI influencer?

To create an AI influencer, choose a visual vibe, generate a character with Picsart Persona, customize the look, and animate it into short-form content. The workflow keeps your identity consistent from the first post to scale.
03

Can I create an AI pet influencer?

Yes. You can design an AI pet influencer with unique traits, outfits, and expressions. Your AI character can be human, animal, or fully fantasy, built around any niche or theme.
04

Can I reuse the same AI character?

Yes. A strong AI character generator supports a persistent identity, so you can reuse, restyle, and evolve the same character across posts while maintaining visual consistency.
05

Is this different from a basic AI character generator?

Yes. A basic AI character generator creates single images. Picsart Persona supports animation, motion, and repeat content, helping you build a scalable virtual presence around one persistent character.
